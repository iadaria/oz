# Subject, the `There is (are)` Construction

## lesson1 Grammar There is (are)

### When it is used

It is used to express the presence or existence of a person or thing in a particular place 📍 or time ⏳, when it is still unknown to the listener.

**Meaning:**

- there is
- is located
- exists
- is present

---

### Translation

**Word order:**

> `There is + Subject + Adverbial modifier.`

In Russian, the sentence often begins with the adverbial of place or time:

- **There is** an apple **on the table**.
  - На столе есть яблоко.
- **There was** a meeting **yesterday**.
  - Вчера была встреча.

---

### Other tenses

The verb to be in the there is construction can be used in different tense forms:

- **there is (are)** — есть, находится
- **there was (were)** — был, находился
- **there has been** — был, имелся
- **there will be** — будет

---

### Negative form with no

> **There is/are + no + noun + adverbial**

- It is formed using no.
- no comes before the noun.
- The noun is used without an article and without the pronoun any.

**There was no meeting yesterday.**
Вчера не было встречи.

| Form                                    | Example                           | Translation            |
| --------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| **There is no + singular noun**         | There is no coffee in the cup.    | В чашке нет кофе.      |
| **There is no + uncountable noun**      | There is no water in the bottle.  | В бутылке нет воды.    |
| **There are no + plural noun**          | There are no chairs in the room.  | В комнате нет стульев. |
| **There was no + singular/uncountable** | There was no meeting yesterday.   | Вчера не было встречи. |
| **There were no + plural noun**         | There were no people here.        | Здесь не было людей.   |
| **There will be no + noun**             | There will be no lesson tomorrow. | Завтра не будет урока. |
