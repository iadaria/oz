# There is / There are — theory with **no**

## Meaning

We use **there is / there are** to say that someone or something exists or is in a particular place or time. In Russian, this structure is often translated as «есть», «имеется», «находится», «был(-и)» or «будет».

- **There is an apple on the table.** — На столе есть яблоко.
- **There was a meeting yesterday.** — Вчера была встреча.

## Affirmative form

Word order:

> **There is/are + noun + place or time expression.**

| Form | Use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **There is** | one person/thing; an uncountable noun | There is a book on the desk. / There is water in the bottle. |
| **There are** | more than one person or thing | There are books on the desk. |

## Negative form with no

In this lesson, make negatives with **no**:

> **There is/are + no + noun + place/time expression.**

**No** comes directly before the noun. It means “not any”, “not a” or “zero”. Do not use **a**, **an**, or **any** after **no**.

| Form | Example | Russian translation |
|---|---|---|
| **There is no + singular noun** | There is no café nearby. | Поблизости нет кафе. |
| **There is no + uncountable noun** | There is no water in the bottle. | В бутылке нет воды. |
| **There are no + plural noun** | There are no chairs in the room. | В комнате нет стульев. |
| **There was no + singular/uncountable noun** | There was no meeting yesterday. | Вчера не было встречи. |
| **There were no + plural noun** | There were no people here. | Здесь не было людей. |
| **There will be no + noun** | There will be no lesson tomorrow. | Завтра не будет урока. |

Example from the original material:

> **There was no meeting yesterday.** — Вчера не было встречи.

## Tenses

The verb **be** changes with the tense:

| Tense | Singular / uncountable | Plural |
|---|---|---|
| Present Simple | there is | there are |
| Past Simple | there was | there were |
| Present Perfect | there has been | there have been |
| Future Simple | there will be | there will be |

- **There has been a problem.** — Была / возникла проблема.
- **There have been several problems.** — Было несколько проблем.
- **There will be a test tomorrow.** — Завтра будет тест.

## Questions

In questions, put **is/are/was/were** before **there**:

- **Is there a bank near here?** — Здесь рядом есть банк?
- **Are there any students in the classroom?** — В классе есть ученики?
- **Was there a meeting yesterday?** — Вчера была встреча?
- **Were there any problems?** — Были какие-нибудь проблемы?

## Key points

- Use **there is** with one thing/person and with uncountable nouns.
- Use **there are** with plural nouns.
- For the negative pattern in this lesson, use **there is/are no + noun**.
- **No** already makes the sentence negative: do not say *There is not no...*.
- Do not use **a/an/any** after **no**: say **There are no books**, not *There are no any books*.

The rule for *no + noun* after *there is/there are* was checked against Cambridge Dictionary: *no* is used before a noun phrase, and *there is/are no + noun* is close in meaning to *there isn’t/aren’t any + noun*. [web:12]
