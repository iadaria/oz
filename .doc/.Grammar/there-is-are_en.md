# There is / There are — theory

## 1. Meaning

We use **there is / there are** to say that someone or something exists or is in a particular place or time. In this structure, **there** usually has no separate translation; the meaning is often «есть», «имеется» or «находится» in Russian.

- **There is an apple on the table.** — На столе есть яблоко.
- **There are two books in the bag.** — В сумке находятся две книги.

## 2. Affirmative form

Word order:

> **There + is/are + noun phrase + place or time expression.**

| Noun type | Form | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Singular countable noun | **There is + a/an + noun** | There is a cat in the room. |
| Plural noun | **There are + plural noun** | There are three cats in the room. |
| Uncountable noun | **There is + uncountable noun** | There is some water in the bottle. |

In spoken English, **there is** is often contracted to **there’s**. The contraction *there’re* is normally avoided in standard English.

## 3. Negative form

Make the negative by adding **not** after **is/are**:

- **There is not** → **There isn’t / There’s not**.
- **There are not** → **There aren’t**.

Examples:

- **There isn’t a meeting today.**
- **There aren’t any chairs here.**
- **There is no milk in the fridge.**
- **There are no tickets left.**

With uncountable nouns, use **there isn’t any + noun**: *There isn’t any coffee.*

## 4. Questions

In a question, put **is/are** before **there**:

- **Is there a pharmacy nearby?**
- **Is there any milk?**
- **Are there any questions?**

Short answers:

- **Yes, there is. / No, there isn’t.**
- **Yes, there are. / No, there aren’t.**

As a standard short answer, do not say *Yes, there’s*.

## 5. Other tenses

The verb **be** changes according to the tense:

| Tense | Singular | Plural |
|---|---|---|
| Present | there is | there are |
| Past | there was | there were |
| Present perfect | there has been | there have been |
| Future | there will be | there will be |

Examples:

- **There was a meeting yesterday.**
- **There were many people at the concert.**
- **There has been an error.**
- **There will be a test tomorrow.**

## 6. There is/are versus a normal subject

Compare:

- **There is an apple on the table.** We introduce the existence of an apple.
- **The apple is on the table.** We talk about a specific apple that is already known.

## 7. Common mistakes

- Incorrect: *There are a book on the desk.* Correct: **There is a book on the desk.**
- Incorrect: *There is two computers.* Correct: **There are two computers.**
- Incorrect: *Is there books here?* Correct: **Are there books here?**
- Incorrect: *There isn’t books.* Correct: **There aren’t any books.**
- With a singular countable noun after **there is**, use an article: **There is a problem**, not *There is problem*.

## 8. Quick check

Complete the sentences with **is, are, was** or **were**:

1. There ___ an apple on the table.
2. There ___ three files in the folder.
3. There ___ a meeting yesterday.
4. ___ there any mistakes?

Answers: **1 is, 2 are, 3 was, 4 Are.**
